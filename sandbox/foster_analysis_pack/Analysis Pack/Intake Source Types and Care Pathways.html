Intake source types and care pathways

Version 0.1Draft for SB Data Owner reviewOctober 2026

Animals in Care counts every animal that came in through a source type marked Incoming in ShelterBuddy. But "incoming" covers very different situations: a stray to rehome, a dog held for its owner, a kitten born in foster, an animal brought in for euthanasia, a wild bird, an animal dead on arrival. Only some of them can ever be fostered. This document shows, from 20 years of records, how each source type is actually used, and asks the SB Data Owner to confirm the SOP behind it, so that Animals in Care and % Animals in Care Fostered count the right animals.

Why it matters

% Animals in Care Fostered = Fostered Animals ÷ Animals in Care. Animals that can never be fostered (dead on arrival, end of life, wildlife) lower the rate without telling us anything about foster.
  • In 2025, 15,017 animals were in care; 944 (6.3%) came in through a source type that is not foster-eligible by SOP (proposed below).
  • % Animals in Care Fostered, 2025: 28.7% on all animals in care; 30.6% on foster-eligible animals only.
  • The effect is uneven between centres: a centre with many dead-on-arrival intakes (Richmond) looks worse than it is.

What decides it today

Each source type has two ShelterBuddy settings (Reference A in Animals in Care):

SettingUsed forSource types
Is IncomingThe only rule today: an incoming source type starts a stay and counts in Animals in Care43 incoming · 16 not
Is DOANot used yet; marks dead-on-arrival source types10

There is no setting for the other situations (end of life, wildlife, short stay). They can only be grouped by source type, which is what we ask the SB Data Owner to confirm.

Notes

  • Counts: distinct animals with at least one record of that source type, January 2006 to September 2026; an animal with intakes of two types counts under both. For service records (not incoming) the count is of animals with that record, not intakes.
  • Fostered animals: animals with a foster placement in the Phase 1 foster data (location history), matched to their latest incoming intake before the foster. For a service record, the fostered animals had no incoming intake at all and were keyed only with that service type (98 animals, mostly Found and Boarding): a source type picked by mistake, and a training point for the SOP.
  • 2026 is January to September only, and an animal that came in recently may not have been fostered yet, so the most recent intakes slightly lower % fostered.
  • Last used and animals since 2023 separate the source types in current use from retired ones; "never" means no record since 2006.
  • Centres and main centre show where a type is used. A type used mostly by one centre (e.g. Euthanasia Request w/ Consent to Rehome: Williams Lake 85%; Ambulance - Wildlife: Vancouver 77%) is a centre-specific practice that the SOP should either make standard or explain.
  • Source type names mix two things: who brought the animal and why. For example, Euthanasia Request, ACO - Euthanasia Request, Ambulance - Euthanasia Request and Humane Officer - Euthanasia Request are one purpose through four channels; the same happens for Owner Surrender, Wildlife and DOA. A clearer SOP would separate the channel (public, ACO, Ambulance, Humane Officer) from the purpose (rehome, hold for owner, end of life, wildlife, DOA).
  • Possible overlaps to review: Owner Surrender and Owner Surrender - Good Samaritan (missing from ShelterBuddy's source list and added by hand); Evacuation and Evacuation Outgoing (never used); the two Community Animal types (no use up to 2025).

How the figures were found

  • Intakes: ShelterBuddy Source History, incoming source types (Is Incoming = true), January 2006 to December 2025. Local extracts.
  • Fostered: the Phase 1 gold foster table (gold_foster.fact_foster_animal_month, from Shelter Location History). A foster placement belongs to the animal's latest incoming intake on or before the foster start. Status names containing "Foster" are not used.
  • Outcomes, status paths and days: the stays in Animals in Care (Status History, rules v0.3).
  • Pathways: proposed from the source type's purpose and confirmed by the data (fostered share, outcomes). They are a proposal until the SB Data Owner confirms them.
