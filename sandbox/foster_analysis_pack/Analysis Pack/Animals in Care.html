How we count animals in care

Version 0.1Draft for Data Owner reviewOctober 2026

The Intake Animal Count looks only at the day an animal came in. Animals in Care counts the animal in every month it was in our care, from the day it came in until the day it left. ShelterBuddy has no single "in care from – to" record, so we build each stay from two histories: the Source History gives the day the animal came in, and the Status History gives the day it left.

The rule in one sentence

An animal counts as in care in a period if at least one of its stays overlaps that period. It counts once per period.
  • Stay: the time from a real intake to the day the animal left our care.
  • Start: the intake date (Date Animal Came In) of a Source History record whose source ShelterBuddy marks as Incoming, as in the Intake Animal Count.
  • End: the date of the first outcome status linked to that intake (Adopted, Redeemed, Transfer Out…). An animal with no outcome yet is still in care.
  • Overlaps the period: in care for at least one day of the month or year, in Vancouver time.
  • Once per period: an animal with several stays in the same month counts as one animal.

Where the dates come from

Every status change in ShelterBuddy is linked to the intake it belongs to. So each intake can be followed from the day the animal came in to its outcome.

Start: Source History

One record each time an animal comes in. Only Incoming sources start a stay (Owner Surrender, Stray, Transfer In, Returns…). Service records such as Lost, Dispatch Information or License do not.

End: Status History

One record each time the animal's status changes, linked to its intake. ShelterBuddy's status settings mark each status as Outgoing (an outcome), In Care, or neither.

The rules depend entirely on how ShelterBuddy's settings classify each source type and each status type. These settings are owned in ShelterBuddy, not by the report: if a type is reclassified there, the stays change with it.

How the end of each stay is found

In order, for each intake:

StepWhat the records showEnd of the stay
1An outcome status is linked to the intakeThe date of the first outcome
2No outcome, and the animal has a later intakeThe date of the later intake. An animal is never in two stays at once
3No outcome, the latest status is In Care, and the intake is 365 days old or lessStill in care: counted up to the report date
4No outcome, and none of the above (an old intake left open, or the latest status is a note)The date of the last status. Never "intake + 365 days", which would invent care time

A later intake also ends an earlier stay whose outcome was recorded after it, so stays never overlap.

When the records are not clean

96.4% of intakes are recorded exactly as expected: one intake, one outcome. For the rest, these rules decide the stay. The full analysis, with examples, is in the Care Timeline Data Quality Report v0.3.

Report period

  • From 1 January 2006, the same start as the foster and intake reports. A stay that started before 2006 counts from January 2006 if the animal was still in care.
  • To the end of the last complete month. An animal still in care is counted up to that date.

Measure: % Animals in Care Fostered

% Animals in Care Fostered = Fostered Animals ÷ Animals in Care, for the same month or year.
  • Fostered Animals: distinct animals in foster for at least one day in the period (Foster Animal Definition).
  • Animals in Care: distinct animals in care for at least one day in the period (this document).

This is the "same population" option in the Intake Animal Count document. Both counts look at the period the same way, so the measure means "the share of animals in our care who were in foster". In 2026 Tilly is in both the top and the bottom of the fraction; with the Intake Animal Count she would be in the top only.

Every fostered animal is also counted as in care: a foster stay that falls outside every stay built from these rules still counts as a care period. So the measure can never go above 100%.

What changed from the earlier proposal

The Intake Animal Count document proposed this measure in outline. Testing the rules against 20 years of records changed some of them:

TopicProposalNow
No outcome, latest status a note (e.g. Returned Home)Cannot be resolved; listed for reviewClosed at that status (step 4). Excluding these would lose real stays; open, they would count the animal in care for ever
No outcome, latest status In CareStill in careStill in care only if the intake is 365 days old or less and the animal has no later intake (steps 2 and 3)
Several intake records at once, like Will'sMerge them into one stayA later intake closes the earlier one. The same result: the animal counts once, with no overlap

Known limitations

  • Some end dates are estimated. 2,036 intakes (0.4%) have no outcome and are closed by rule; most are from before 2013.
  • Before 2013, status dates have no time. A stay may start or end a few hours off, which can move it into a neighbouring day, but rarely a different month.
  • Records are not corrected in ShelterBuddy. Duplicate intakes and second outcomes stay as they are; the rules decide how they are counted.
  • Returns recorded on the old intake count as a stay only when they end with their own outcome (292 intakes).

StayStayStartUtc to StayEndUtc (NULL = still in care); StayNo 2, 3… for returns on the same intake; EndReason = Outcome, NextIntake, LastStatus or StillInCare

PeriodVancouver dates from 1 January 2006 to EOMONTH(GETDATE(), -1)
MeasureAnimals in Care = DISTINCTCOUNT(AnimalId) over stays with IsStayRow = 1, IsExcluded = 0, StayStart ≤ period end and COALESCE(StayEnd, report date) ≥ period start
