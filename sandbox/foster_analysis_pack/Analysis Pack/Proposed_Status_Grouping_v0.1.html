04Findings from the cross-reference

F1 — ID 112 is assigned to three different groups at once "Awaiting Vet Check / Health Exam" (ID 112) appears in the In Foster, Available for Adoption, and Hold substatus lists, each time annotated "(Consolidate with 7 Under Vet Care)." The proposal doesn't resolve which group it actually belongs to — or whether "Under Vet Care" (ID 7) is itself meant to survive as a group, since ID 7 is never given its own final bucket either.
F2 — Likely transcription error: "49 Awaiting Final Disposition" The Hold substatus table lists both "49 Emergency Boarding" and "49 Awaiting Final Disposition" — reusing ID 49 for two different names. status.csv confirms ID 49 = Emergency Boarding and ID 79 = Awaiting Final Disposition. This summary maps the second row to ID 79 based on the name match, but the source document itself should be corrected, not silently trusted.
F3 — ID 27 name mismatch The Review to Delete/Hide table lists "27 Stray in Foster." status.csv shows ID 27 = "Escaped." Either the source document has the wrong ID, or this export is stale relative to current SB configuration — unresolved, not guessed at here.
F4 — "65 Owner Relinquishment" is a taxonomy-category change, not a grouping decision The source document recommends moving this out of Status entirely and into Source — a structurally different kind of change than every other row in this document, which only reassigns statuses among themselves.
F5 — 12 of the 87 IDs named across the proposal's lists don't exist in this export IDs 11, 14, 41, 58, 73, 82, 108, 129, 134, 141, 153, 154, 162, 163, 74 — referenced in Michelle & Cynthia's review or its substatus lists — are absent from status.csv's 120 rows. Consistent with the already-documented "87 vs. 67/120" reconciliation gap (BRD §4, §12): this export is not a complete picture of every status the prior review had in front of it.
F6 — "Information" bucket is both struck through and kept The summary line in the source document reads "~~45 Information~~" (strikethrough), implying removal — but item 6 of the detailed list still describes it in full ("Community S/N, dispatch, license sales, L&F; animals not in care but need SB#"). This document keeps Information in the 6-bucket count per the earlier-confirmed BRD framing, but the contradiction itself is unresolved in the source and should be confirmed with Michelle directly.
F7 — Not Defined is the largest single group At 50 statuses (42% of the export), "not mentioned at all" is bigger than any individual proposed bucket, including Delete/Hide (28). A consolidation proposal that's silent on more statuses than it explicitly addresses is evidence the 87/120-status review wasn't actually exhaustive — independent of whether any individual grouping choice above is right.
Diagnostic evidence only — this document does not propose where a Not Defined status should go, or resolve F1–F6. It feeds Business Question 7 of BRD_SB_Status_Source_Operational_Pathway_v0.1.html ("How does Michelle & Cynthia's proposed consolidation compare against the empirical evidence?"), moving that question from Not Started toward Partial.
