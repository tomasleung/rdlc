Methodology, definitions & data quality

Transparent definitions and limitations, per BRD v0.2 §08 and §14.

Primary reporting metrics

Animals in Care

Distinct animals whose valid incoming-to-outgoing care episode overlaps at least one day of the reporting month.

Fostered Animals

Distinct Animals in Care that overlap a qualifying foster stay for at least one day in the reporting month. Reconciles exactly to Phase 1 at the province-wide level.

% Animals in Care Fostered

Fostered Animals ÷ Animals in Care × 100. Distinct from Phase 1's "% Animals in Foster," which divides by Intake Animals instead — the two are not the same statistic.

Foster Days

Calendar days of qualifying foster care allocated to the effective Centre and Animal Type during each dated segment.

Important interpretation notes

  • Distinct counting: animals are counted once per reporting period and dimensional group.
  • No simple summation: Centre/Region/Type subtotals must not be added to derive the province-wide total.
  • Descriptive, not performance: higher or lower utilization is descriptive only. It does not establish good or poor performance, capacity sufficiency, unmet demand, better outcomes, or an operational target.
