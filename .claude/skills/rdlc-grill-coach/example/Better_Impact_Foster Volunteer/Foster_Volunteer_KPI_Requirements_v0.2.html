01  What Better Impact actually tracks

Every measure below is built from a volunteer's status in Better Impact. Here's the real lifecycle, left to right:

Applicant
Just applied, not yet reviewed.
→
In Process
Being reviewed/onboarded.
→
Active / Accepted
Approved — a real foster volunteer.
→
Inactive
Temporary pause — may come back.
Archived
Permanent exit.

Everything below is measuring movement between these five statuses — mainly who's currently Active/Accepted, and who's moved to Archived.

An assumption we're making Better Impact doesn't appear to keep a full history of status changes — it only keeps the most recent date for each status a volunteer has held. So if a volunteer ever went from Inactive back to Active/Accepted, the only "Active" date we can see is their most recent one, not their original one. Any measure that asks "who was already active as of some past date" could therefore miss someone who was active earlier, took a break, and came back — especially if that break-and-return happened close to the date being checked. We don't yet know how often this actually happens; we're flagging it as a risk, not treating it as solved.

02  The measures

Our default preference Wherever possible, we use Better Impact alone. Of the 10 measures below, 8 need only Better Impact. Only the Engagement pair (rows 9–10) requires joining to ShelterBuddy, because Better Impact has no field for which specific animal a volunteer fostered — that fact lives only in ShelterBuddy.
KeyPriorityAnalysis CategoryKPIDefinitionSource System
1P1Retention Active Fosters Over 1 Year How many volunteers currently marked Active/Accepted have held that status for more than one year? Better Impact
2P1Retention Active Fosters How many volunteers are currently marked Active/Accepted, regardless of how long they've held that status? Better Impact
3P1Retention % Active Fosters Over 1 Year Of all volunteers currently marked Active/Accepted (2), what percentage have held that status for more than a year (1)? Better Impact
4P1Retention Fosters Archived How many volunteers were moved to Archived status during the period (year, quarter, or month)? Better Impact
5P1Retention Not Yet Archived at Start of Period How many volunteers had already become Active/Accepted, and had not yet been moved to Archived, as of the start of that same period? Better Impact
6P1Retention New Foster Created How many new volunteer records were first created (Applicant) during the period? Better Impact
7P1Retention Archived–to–New Ratio Compared to how many new volunteers joined during the period (6), how many were moved to Archived during that same period (4)? Better Impact
8P1Retention % Fosters Archived Of the volunteers we already had at the start of the period (5), what percentage were moved to Archived by the end of it (4)? Better Impact
9P2Engagement Active Fosters With a Recent Foster Placement How many currently Active/Accepted volunteers have taken an animal into foster care within the last 90 days? Better Impact + ShelterBuddy
10P2Engagement % Active Fosters With a Recent Foster Placement Of all currently Active/Accepted volunteers (2), what percentage have taken an animal into foster care in the last 90 days (9)? Better Impact + ShelterBuddy

Note the same numerator (1 Archived) gives two very different-looking results — 100% (row 7) vs. 50% (row 8) — depending on which base it's compared against. That's why rows 5–8 exist as separate, named measures rather than one single "Attrition %."

Also note: in this example, Sarah's break-and-return (footnote above) happened to land before Jan 1, 2025, so row 5 still counts her correctly. If her return had instead happened during 2025, she could have been wrongly left out of row 5 for that year — the assumption above isn't just theoretical, this is exactly where it would bite.

04  Dimensions — coming next

Not in this version, by design Member Tenure (grouping Active/Accepted volunteers by how long they've held that status) and Tenure at Exit (grouping Archived volunteers by how long they lasted before leaving) are groupings used to break the measures above down further — they are not measures in their own right, so they don't belong in the table above. They'll get their own section in the next version.
Still open before any of this is final Data Owner confirmation of the whole set; whether "Active/Accepted Date" is a real, dedicated field in Better Impact or something we'd need to approximate another way; how often the Inactive→Active break-and-return pattern actually happens; the ShelterBuddy–Better Impact join key (blocks rows 9–10); the 90-day window choice; and how far back Better Impact's own records go.
