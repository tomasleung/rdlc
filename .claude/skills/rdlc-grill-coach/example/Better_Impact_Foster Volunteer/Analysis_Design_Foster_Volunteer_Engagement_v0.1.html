Layer 5 — Decision insight: what's worth focusing on

Translation rule, not a new method Every insight above may only be phrased as "X is worth investigating as related to Y" — never "X is the cause/driver/constraint behind Y." This applies even where a correlation is strong: strength of relationship is not proof of cause (rule 7).

Layer 6 — Action: explicitly out of scope here

No recommended action is drafted in this register. A decision — recruit, intervene on retention, re-engage an under-used Active pool — belongs to the BRD's named Decision Owner (Lindsay, pending confirmation), using the evidence above. This document stops at Layer 5.
