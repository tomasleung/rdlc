01Why two metrics instead of one

Lindsay's original draft bundled three separable concepts — current status, tenure, and utilization — into a single count. Splitting them answers her actual question ("are my recruitment/onboarding/support efforts producing volunteers who stay and actually foster") in a way the original single number structurally couldn't.

Member Tenure

Definition
Time since Date Joined, bucketed into <1yr, 1–2yr, 2yr+, cut by current Status (Applicant / In Process / Active / Inactive / Archived).
Formula
DATEDIFF(day, Date_Joined, Today) / 365, bucketed
Grain
One row per volunteer, current snapshot.
Source
Better Impact — Date Joined field (confirmed to exist; a pure membership-duration fact, no ambiguity in what it measures).
Type
Descriptive — membership-duration fact only.
Permitted conclusion
How long volunteers typically stay on the roster, by current status.
Prohibited conclusion
Does not by itself indicate engagement, satisfaction, or whether the volunteer is actually fostering — see Foster Utilization Rate below for that.

Foster Utilization Rate

Definition
% of volunteers in a given Tenure/Status bucket who have at least one ShelterBuddy AnimalID+PersonID placement within a chosen recency window (proposed default: last 12 months).
Formula (primary)
COUNT(PersonID with ≥1 AnimalID link in last 12mo) ÷ COUNT(PersonID in bucket)
Grain
Tenure bucket × Status, current snapshot.
Source
Primary cut: ShelterBuddy AnimalID+PersonID placement history — reliable, not subject to Better Impact's confirmed status-lag problem.
Secondary cut
Active-status-denominator version (Better Impact Active ÷ fostered in <1yr) — kept as a clearly-labeled secondary view specifically because it exposes the ghost-volunteer/admin-lag gap rather than hiding it. Never shown without this label.
Type
Descriptive utilization measure.
Permitted conclusion
What share of a given tenure/status segment is actively delivering foster placements.
Prohibited conclusion
Not a performance score for individual volunteers; not a capacity-sufficiency measure on its own (that's the separate Need-vs-Capacity question from the Better Impact access-request email).
Starting three, confirmed 2026-11-18 Rather than defining the % directly, the numerator and denominator are each their own named, standalone KPI first — auditable on their own, not just intermediate math inside a ratio. This is the build order: Count 1, then Count 2, then Count 1 ÷ Count 2.

1. Active Fosters Over 1 Year

Business definition
How many foster volunteers currently marked Active in Better Impact have been with BC SPCA for more than one year, counting from the date they originally joined? This is Lindsay's original ask, precisely defined — anchored to Date Joined, not to the last status change date (which would understate anyone who ever went Inactive and came back).
Implementation note
COUNT(Status = Active AND tenure since Date Joined > 1yr)
Grain
Current snapshot.
Source
Better Impact only — Status and Date Joined.
Layer
Layer 1 — Admin Roster Hygiene (per Lindsay's own original brief).

2. Active Fosters

Business definition
How many foster volunteers are currently marked Active in Better Impact, regardless of how long they've been with us? The denominator for KPI 3 below, and a useful number on its own — the current size of the working roster.
Implementation note
COUNT(Status = Active)
Grain
Current snapshot.
Source
Better Impact only — Status.
Layer
Layer 1 — Admin Roster Hygiene.

3. % Active Fosters Over 1 Year

Business definition
Of all foster volunteers currently Active (KPI 2), what percentage have been with BC SPCA for more than one year (KPI 1)? A quick, Layer 1 pulse-check on the roster's tenure mix — it answers "who's on the roster," not "who's actually fostering."
Implementation note
KPI 1 ÷ KPI 2
Grain
Current snapshot, all Active volunteers.
Source
Derived from KPIs 1 and 2 — Better Impact only. No ShelterBuddy join needed, so all three are buildable as soon as Better Impact access is granted, independent of the join-key question in the Discovery document.
Layer
Layer 1 — Admin Roster Hygiene. Not the operational signal on its own.
Permitted conclusion
The tenure mix of the currently Active roster, and how that mix is trending over time.
Prohibited conclusion
Do not read a rising % as proof retention is improving on its own. Because Active status can lag real behavior (ghost-volunteer risk, already confirmed in both Lindsay's brief and Better Impact's own documentation), a rising share could mean genuine long-term retention, or it could simply mean fewer new volunteers have joined recently — changing the mix without retention itself changing. Always read this alongside KPIs 1 and 2 themselves, and eventually the Foster Utilization Rate, before concluding anything.
KPIs 4 & 5 — blocked, unlike 1–3 These need the ShelterBuddy–Better Impact join key, still unconfirmed in the Discovery document. Defined now so the shape is ready the moment that's resolved — not buildable yet.

4. Active Fosters With a Recent Foster Placement

Business definition
How many currently Active foster volunteers have taken at least one animal into foster care within the last 90 days? Answers "how many of our Active fosters can we realistically count on right now" — tenure (KPIs 1–3) can't answer that; only actual placement activity can.
Implementation note
COUNT(Status = Active AND ≥1 ShelterBuddy AnimalID+PersonID placement within last 90 days)
Grain
Current snapshot.
Source
Better Impact (Status) joined to ShelterBuddy (AnimalID+PersonID placement history) — requires the join key confirmed.
Layer
Layer 2 — Operational Foster Signal.
Window
90 days — proposed default, not yet confirmed by the Data Owner. Chosen to match a realistic placement-plus-gap cycle and a standard quarterly cadence, and to stay consistent with the 90-day window already in Lindsay's own original Layer 2 proposal. Not yet validated against BC SPCA's actual distribution of gaps between a volunteer's placements — worth a sanity check once ShelterBuddy data is joined, before treating 90 days as settled.

5. % Active Fosters With a Recent Foster Placement

Business definition
Of all currently Active foster volunteers (KPI 2), what percentage have taken at least one animal into foster care within the last 90 days (KPI 4)? This is the number that puts a precise size on the ghost-volunteer problem — a low % here means "Active" status is substantially overstating real, usable capacity.
Implementation note
KPI 4 ÷ KPI 2
Grain
Current snapshot, all Active volunteers.
Source
Derived from KPIs 2 and 4.
Layer
Layer 2 — Operational Foster Signal. This is the reality check KPI 3 (tenure mix) explicitly cannot provide on its own.
Permitted conclusion
What share of the current Active roster is realistically available for placement right now, based on actual recent behavior rather than administrative status.
Prohibited conclusion
Not a performance score for individual volunteers, and not by itself a capacity-sufficiency answer — that's the separate Need-vs-Capacity question from the Better Impact access-request email. A volunteer with no placement in 90 days isn't necessarily failing; see the Industry Standard Reference document's turnover-reasons research before assuming cause.

9. Foster Roster Size, Year-End (Approximate)

Business definition
For each past year, approximately how many foster volunteers had joined BC SPCA and had not yet been permanently archived by the end of that year? Built to trend alongside the existing 20-year Animals-in-Foster chart (Intake Animals, Foster Animals, % Animals in Foster) — pairing it against this roster-size estimate lets us tell whether a change in % Animals in Foster over time was driven by more or fewer available foster volunteers, versus just a change in intake volume.
Implementation note
COUNT(Date Joined ≤ Dec 31 of year Y AND NOT Archived as of Dec 31 of year Y)
Grain
One value per year, trended across available history.
Source
Better Impact — Date Joined and Date Archived only, same two persistent fields used for KPI 7's fix. Limited to however far back BC SPCA's actual Better Impact records go — unconfirmed, new question added to the Discovery document.
Layer
Layer 1 — Admin Roster Hygiene, approximate historical reconstruction.
Permitted conclusion
An approximate historical capacity-pool size, trended, for pairing with the existing animal-side chart in a capacity narrative.
Prohibited conclusion
This is not a true historical Active-status count. It cannot distinguish a volunteer who was genuinely fostering that year from one who was sitting Inactive the whole time, because Better Impact has no retrievable status history (confirmed earlier in this folder's Discovery and Technical Integration documents). This approximation assumes the real-world rate of Active→Inactive→Active cycling is low enough not to matter — Tomas's own estimate is under 3%, stated as an assumption, not a verified figure — and should be checked against real data once available (e.g., by counting how many currently-Active volunteers show any evidence of a prior Inactive period, if the admin confirms that evidence is recoverable at all).

02Combined view — the actual operational story

Crossing both metrics is what answers the real question neither could alone:

Never fosteredFostered, not in last 12moFostered within last 12mo
Active, <1yr tenureNew, not yet converted—Converting well
Active, >1yr tenureLong-tenured, never used — onboarding/matching problemLong-tenured, gone quiet — re-engagement candidateHealthy core
InactiveNever used before going inactiveUsed, then stopped — real attrition—
ArchivedRejected/never startedUsed, then left — the real "lost capacity" group—

This separates "never converted" (onboarding problem) from "converted then lost" (retention problem) — two different operational fixes that "Retention = # active >1yr" could never distinguish from each other.
