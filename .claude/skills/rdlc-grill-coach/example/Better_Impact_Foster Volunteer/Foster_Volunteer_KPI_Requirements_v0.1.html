Our default preference Wherever possible, we use Better Impact alone — it's simpler, faster to build, and doesn't depend on a data link that isn't confirmed yet. Of the 9 measures below, 7 need only Better Impact. Only the Utilization pair requires joining to ShelterBuddy, because Better Impact has no field for which specific animal a volunteer fostered — that fact lives only in ShelterBuddy.
How to read Priority and Relates To P1 = directly answers one of your two original requests (Retention / Attrition) and is buildable right now. P2 = supporting detail that strengthens a P1 measure, also buildable now. P3 = a measure we're proposing beyond your original ask, because we think it matters — but it's blocked pending the ShelterBuddy join. "Relates To" names which original request each measure traces back to, or flags it as a new idea.
Priority Analysis Category KPI Name Business Definition Relates To Note Source System Risk
P1 Retention Active Fosters Over 1 Year How many foster volunteers currently marked Active have been with BC SPCA for more than one year, from their original join date. Retention (your original ask) This is your original "Retention" ask, precisely defined. Better Impact Current snapshot only — can't be used for historical trending on its own (see KPI 9 for that).
P1 Retention Active Fosters How many foster volunteers are currently marked Active, regardless of how long they've been with us. Retention (supporting base) The base/denominator number for the % below. Better Impact Better Impact's Active status can lag real behavior — someone who stopped fostering may not be archived promptly.
P1 Retention % Active Fosters Over 1 Year Of all current Active fosters, what percentage have been with us more than a year? Retention (your original ask) A rising % can mean real retention improving, or just fewer new recruits — always read with the raw counts above, not alone. Better Impact Mix effect: this % can't tell you on its own whether it moved due to retention or recruitment volume.
P2 Retention Member Tenure (dimension) How long each foster volunteer has been with us, grouped into bands (under 1 year, 1–2 years, 2+ years). Retention (supporting detail) A breakdown, not a standalone number — used to group the other KPIs. Better Impact Same lag risk as above.
P3 Engagement Active Fosters With a Recent Foster Placement How many currently Active fosters have actually taken an animal into foster care within the last 90 days? New — beyond your original ask Answers "who can we really count on right now" — tenure alone can't answer this. Better Impact + ShelterBuddy Needs a confirmed link between your Better Impact record and your ShelterBuddy record — not yet confirmed to exist. Better Impact itself does not track which animal was fostered; that fact only lives in ShelterBuddy, which is why this one measure can't be Better Impact-only. 90-day window is our proposed default, not yet your confirmed choice.
P3 Engagement % Active Fosters With a Recent Foster Placement Of all current Active fosters, what percentage actually fostered an animal in the last 90 days? New — beyond your original ask Puts a real number on how many "Active" fosters are genuinely available — not a judgment on anyone, just a measure of recent activity. Better Impact + ShelterBuddy Same join-key dependency as above.
P1 Retention Fosters Archived How many foster volunteers were permanently archived during a given year or quarter? Attrition (your original ask) This is your original "Attrition" ask, using Archived specifically, not Inactive (which is a temporary pause). Better Impact Better Impact keeps no change history — if someone was archived more than once (archived, reinstated, archived again), only the most recent event is visible.
P1 Retention Fosters Not Yet Archived at Start of Period How many foster volunteers had joined, and had not yet been archived, as of the start of that same year or quarter? Attrition (supporting base) A stand-in for "how many were available to lose" — the real basis for an attrition rate. Better Impact This is a deliberate substitute for a true historical "Active" count, which Better Impact cannot provide (it has no retained status history). It measures "not yet gone," not confirmed engagement.
P1 Retention % Fosters Archived Of the fosters who were available at the start of the period, what percentage were archived by the end of it? Attrition (your original ask) The actual attrition rate, trended by year or quarter. Better Impact Inherits both risks above.
P2 Retention Tenure at Exit (dimension) How long each archived volunteer had been with us before leaving. Attrition (supporting detail) Separates "left early" (onboarding problem) from "left after years" (a different, more natural kind of exit) — a breakdown, not a standalone number. Better Impact Same multi-archive-event limitation as Fosters Archived.
P2 Retention Foster Roster Size, Year-End (Approximate) For each past year, approximately how many foster volunteers had joined and not yet been archived by that year's end — intended to sit alongside our existing 20-year Animals-in-Foster chart as a capacity-side comparison. New — beyond your original ask Lets us ask whether a change in % Animals in Foster over time was about more/fewer available fosters, or just intake volume changing. Better Impact An approximation, not a true historical Active count — it assumes volunteers rarely cycle Active→Inactive→Active (our own estimate: under 3%, not yet verified). Also limited by how far back BC SPCA's actual Better Impact records go, which we haven't confirmed.
Still open before any of this is final Data Owner confirmation of the whole set; the ShelterBuddy–Better Impact join key (blocks the two Utilization rows); the 90-day window choice; and how far back Better Impact's own records go (limits the Historical Trend row). None of this is being treated as settled until you've reviewed it.
