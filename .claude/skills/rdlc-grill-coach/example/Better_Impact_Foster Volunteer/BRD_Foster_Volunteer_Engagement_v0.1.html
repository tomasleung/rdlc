01 · Executive summary

The Foster Volunteer Member Support team cannot currently see, in one place, how BC SPCA's foster-volunteer pool is performing. Better Impact (the volunteer-management system) has its own reporting, but it is hard to locate, cannot be compared against ShelterBuddy's animal-side data, and — until this effort — had no agreed definition of which measures actually matter. Today, any combined view requires manually exporting from both systems and reconciling them by hand.

This BRD governs a set of 10 volunteer-side KPIs and 2 supporting dimensions, built entirely from Better Impact's own status lifecycle (Applicant → In Process → Active/Accepted → Inactive / Archived), replacing the Data Owner's two original, imprecisely-specified measures ("Retention," "Attrition") with named, decomposed, independently auditable definitions. It also governs a framework for reading these volunteer-side measures side by side with ShelterBuddy's existing animal-side history (Intake Animals, Foster Animals, % Animals in Foster), so that capacity and demand can finally be compared.

The principal outputs are: (1) the KPI and dimension set itself (Section 8); (2) a documented method for combining it with the animal side to support specific operational decisions (Section 6 of the companion Requirements document); and (3) a plan to build a historical volunteer-pool trend to sit alongside the animal side's existing 20-year history, from which a forward operating benchmark can eventually be set.

Main interpretation boundary
No measure in this BRD may be read as a performance score for an individual volunteer, as a confirmed cause of attrition, or as a fully automated cross-system report. Several measures depend on fields in Better Impact (Active/Accepted Date, Archived Date) whose existence and reliability are not yet confirmed by the Better Impact admin — see Sections 9 and 12.

02 · Business problem

Current condition. The Foster Volunteer Member Support team manages volunteer records in Better Impact and animal placements in ShelterBuddy. No report or process currently combines the two. Any question that touches both — for example, "do we have enough fosters for our current intake volume" — requires someone to manually export data from each system and compare it by hand.

Information/decision gap. Before this effort, the team had two informally stated measures — "Retention" (fosters active more than a year) and "Attrition" (fosters archived per year/quarter) — neither with a documented formula, population, or grain. Both were found, on inspection, to be ambiguous in ways that would produce inconsistent answers depending on who built the report (see the companion Discovery KPI Definitions document for the specific ambiguities found and resolved).

People/process affected. Lindsay (Foster Volunteer Member Support) is the primary person affected; any Foster Manager who needs to decide whether to prioritize recruitment versus retention action is a secondary consumer.

Operational/governance consequence. Without defined measures, retention and capacity decisions have been made without a consistent, auditable basis. Without a cross-system view, capacity (volunteer side) and demand (animal side) cannot be compared at all, so it isn't possible to tell whether a given period's foster shortfall is a recruitment problem, a retention problem, or simply a demand spike.

Why existing reports are insufficient. Better Impact's own standard reports are difficult to locate within the product and do not support cross-product analysis. Better Impact also retains no change history for a volunteer's status — only the current status and, per BC SPCA's own custom tracking, a date per status last reached — which further limits what any native report could show even if it were easy to find.

04 · Current state

  • Existing process/artifact: manual export from Better Impact and from ShelterBuddy, reconciled by hand; no standing report combines them.
  • Existing trusted definitions: none formally governed before this effort. Lindsay's own two draft measures ("Retention," "Attrition") were the only prior starting point, and both were found ambiguous on inspection.
  • Current sources: Better Impact (volunteer status and status dates); ShelterBuddy (animal intake, foster placement, and the existing Intake Animals / Foster Animals / % Animals in Foster history).
  • Known limitations: Better Impact retains no change history for status (only the current status, plus a date-per-status-last-reached that BC SPCA tracks via its own custom fields — not yet confirmed as a reliable, dedicated field by the admin); Better Impact has no scheduling/automation for reports at any tier; API and raw-data-report access is gated to a tier BC SPCA's current subscription has not been confirmed to hold.
  • Manual effort/fragmentation: as described in Section 2 — every cross-system question today requires a manual export-and-compare step.
  • Previous phases that must remain authoritative: the Phase 1 (ShelterBuddy counters, initiative 01) animal-side measures and the Centre & Animal Type Utilization (CATU, initiative 02) "Animals in Care" population measure are both authoritative on the animal side and are not redefined by this BRD. Note: Phase 1's own "% Animals in Foster" measure is itself still contested within this project (initiative 01) — this BRD uses it as-is, flags it is not yet confirmed, and does not attempt to resolve that separate open question.

07 · Analytical approach

  • Reporting grain: one row per Better Impact volunteer record for KPIs 1–8 and Dimensions 11–12; one row per ShelterBuddy animal placement for the animal-side pairing in Section 6 of the Requirements document.
  • Population: all Better Impact records that have reached at least Applicant status; measures scoped to Active/Accepted or Archived are stated explicitly per KPI (Section 8).
  • Entity/event identifiers: Better Impact member record (identifier not yet profiled); ShelterBuddy AnimalID+PersonID placement link (existence of a reliable link between the two systems is unconfirmed — Section 12).
  • Time zone: assumed Pacific, not yet explicitly confirmed.
  • Interval boundaries: calendar year, quarter, or month, Data Owner's choice; "start of period" = first calendar day of the chosen interval.
  • Effective-dated treatment: Active/Accepted Date and Archived Date are used as point-in-time proxies for "was this person already a foster, and not yet gone, as of a past date." This is an approximation, not a true historical status snapshot — Better Impact retains no change history, only the most recent date reached per status (see Section 12's assumptions).
  • Distinct-count keys: one count per volunteer member record; one count per ShelterBuddy placement event where used.
  • Additive/non-additive behaviour: Active Fosters (KPI 2) and Not Yet Archived at Start of Period (KPI 5) are point-in-time snapshots — not additive across periods. Fosters Archived (KPI 4) and New Foster Created (KPI 6) are additive within a stated period only.
  • Historical restatement: not supported. If a volunteer's status was overwritten by a later reactivation or reinstatement before this BRD's extraction began, the earlier period cannot be corrected retroactively.
  • Current/open record treatment: Active and Inactive are both treated as "not yet gone" for KPI 5's purposes (Inactive is not a disqualifier); Archived is treated as terminal, pending the admin confirming whether reinstatement from Archived is actually possible (Section 12).
Worked example required by this standard
A 5-person illustrative dataset (Tom, Sarah, Maria, David, Priya — see Appendix A and SCD1_Member_Lifecycle_Validation_Sample_v0.1.csv, same folder) was built specifically to validate every formula in Section 8 by hand before this BRD was drafted, including the one case (a reactivation straddling a period boundary) where the stated assumption in Section 12 would visibly break the count if it occurred at the wrong time.

08 · Business definitions and KPI contracts

Full field-by-field contracts (business definition, formula, grain, source, layer, permitted/prohibited conclusion) are maintained in the companion Foster_Volunteer_Discovery_KPI_Definitions_v0.1.html (internal/technical) and summarized in business language in Foster_Volunteer_KPI_Requirements_v0.3.html (Data Owner-facing), both in this same folder. This BRD governs that set by reference rather than duplicating every field; the cards below are a condensed summary.

1–3 · Active Fosters Over 1 Year / Active Fosters / % Active Fosters Over 1 Year

How many current Active/Accepted volunteers have held that status over a year (1); how many are Active/Accepted at all, any tenure (2); the ratio of the two (3).

(1) COUNT(Status=Active AND tenure>1yr) · (2) COUNT(Status=Active) · (3) = (1) ÷ (2)

Grain: current snapshot. Source: Better Impact. Owner: Tomas Leung (proposed), pending Lindsay's confirmation.

4–6, 8 · Fosters Archived / Not Yet Archived at Start of Period / New Foster Created / % Fosters Archived

How many were Archived in the period (4); how many had already become Active and weren't yet Archived as of the period's start, including anyone currently Inactive (5); how many new records started in the period (6); the true attrition rate, (4)÷(5) (8).

(4) COUNT(ArchivedDate in period) · (5) COUNT(ActiveDate≤start AND not yet Archived by start) · (6) COUNT(ApplicantDate in period) · (8) = (4) ÷ (5)

Grain: period-based count (4, 6) or start-of-period snapshot (5). Source: Better Impact.

7 · Archived–to–New Ratio

Archived during the period, divided by new volunteers who joined during that same period — a loss-versus-growth comparison, distinct from the attrition rate (8), which compares loss against the people already on hand.

(7) = (4) ÷ (6)

Grain: period-based. Source: Better Impact.

9–10 · Active Fosters With a Recent Foster Placement / %

How many currently Active/Accepted volunteers have taken an animal into foster care within the last 90 days (9); what share of all Active/Accepted volunteers that represents (10).

(9) COUNT(Status=Active AND ≥1 SB placement in last 90 days) · (10) = (9) ÷ (2)

Grain: current snapshot. Source: Better Impact + ShelterBuddy — requires the join key confirmed (Section 12). 90-day window proposed, not yet confirmed.

11 · Time as an Active Foster (dimension)

Groups Active/Accepted volunteers by how long they've held that status (e.g. under 1yr / 1–2yr / 2+yr) — not a standalone count, used to break down KPIs 1–3.

Band boundaries are proposed defaults, to be built as an adjustable setting, not hard-coded (per the Requirements document, Section 4).

12 · Time With Us Before Leaving (dimension)

Groups Archived volunteers by how long they had been Active/Accepted before leaving — separates early exits (possible onboarding issue) from long-tenure exits (a different, more natural kind of exit).

Same band-boundary note as Dimension 11.

Approval status
All 10 KPIs and 2 dimensions above are proposed, not yet confirmed by Lindsay as Data Owner. Formulas are validated against the Appendix A worked example, not yet against a real Better Impact export.

10 · Data-design requirements

  • Required output grain: one row per Better Impact member record, with derived period-based counts per Section 7.
  • Effective start/end semantics: Active/Accepted Date and Archived Date act as the only available start/end markers; no interim Inactive period is reliably bounded.
  • Event precedence/tie-breaking: not yet defined — depends on whether Better Impact's underlying date fields can hold same-day multiple transitions; flagged as a dependency pending admin confirmation.
  • Source identifiers retained for audit: the Better Impact member identifier must be retained on every derived row; no equivalent requirement yet defined for ShelterBuddy placement records pending the join-key confirmation.
  • Change detection: not available — Better Impact exposes no change log; any change-detection approach would have to be built as a BC SPCA-side snapshot comparison, which is itself subject to the same overwrite risk described in Section 12.
  • Current-row handling: the member's current Status always reflects "now"; all period-based KPIs (4–8) must derive their own point-in-time logic from the date fields rather than relying on current Status.
  • Conflict handling / unknown-unmapped treatment: Applicant and In Process records are explicitly excluded from the "foster" population used in KPIs 1–3 and 5 — they have never been an active foster. Whether a "Total Members, all statuses" base measure should also be built is an open design question, not yet decided (see Appendix D).
  • Lineage/traceability: every KPI in Section 8 traces to a specific business question in Section 6; every formula was hand-validated against Appendix A before being included in this BRD.
  • Reconciliation controls: re-run the Appendix A worked example against the formulas in Section 8 any time a formula changes, before republishing either this BRD or the companion documents.
A status-row change in Better Impact (e.g. Active → Inactive) must not be read as a new foster-placement event — it is an administrative status change only, unrelated to any specific animal placement.

11 · Scope

In scope

  • The 10 KPIs and 2 dimensions defined in Section 8, built entirely from Better Impact.
  • The business-question-to-decision mapping in Section 6 (and the Requirements document's Section 5).
  • A documented, person-read (not system-automated) framework for comparing the above against existing ShelterBuddy animal-side history (Requirements document, Section 6).
  • A worked, hand-validated example proving every formula computes as intended (Appendix A).

Deferred to a named future phase

  • Animals in Care as a second denominator alongside the current Intake-Animals-based % Animals in Foster, once that measure is confirmed and available for this pairing. (Per Tomas's explicit instruction, this deferred item is intentionally not stated in the Data-Owner-facing Requirements document — it is recorded here only, in this governed BRD, per the BRD Content Standard's requirement to state deferred scope explicitly.)
  • An automated, system-to-system join between Better Impact and ShelterBuddy, pending confirmation that a reliable join key exists at all (Section 12).
  • A historical volunteer-pool trend view to sit alongside the existing 20-year animal-side chart, pending confirmation of how far back Better Impact's own records actually go.
  • A forward operating benchmark for pool size, pending the historical view above being built.

Out of scope

  • Individual volunteer performance evaluation — no measure in this BRD may be read as a performance score for a named person.
  • Causal attribution of why any individual volunteer left.
  • Setting an actual recruitment headcount target.
  • Real-time or live reporting — Better Impact has no native scheduling at any tier.
  • Power BI/report build — this BRD is a Step 0 intake artifact; semantic model and report design are later RDLC OS stages, not covered here.

12 · Assumptions, constraints, dependencies, and accepted limitations

Assumptions

  • "Active/Accepted Date" and "Archived Date" are real, dedicated, reliable fields in Better Impact — not yet confirmed.
  • The rate of Active→Inactive→Active cycling is low (Tomas's own estimate, under 3%) — stated as an assumption, not verified.
  • Archived is a terminal status with no path back — not yet confirmed; if reinstatement is possible, it inherits the same overwrite risk as Inactive→Active reactivation.

Constraints

  • Better Impact has no scheduling or automation for any report, at any tier.
  • Better Impact's JSON API and raw-data reporting are gated to a subscription tier BC SPCA's current holding has not been confirmed to include.

Dependencies

  • Better Impact admin confirmation: does "Active/Accepted Date" exist as a dedicated field; does "Archived Date" behave the same way; can a volunteer go Inactive→Active again and is any trace visible afterward; can a volunteer be un-Archived and is any trace visible afterward; does the 5-status lifecycle match BC SPCA's actual configured account; how far back do records go.
  • Confirmation of a reliable join key between a Better Impact member record and a ShelterBuddy AnimalID+PersonID record (blocks KPIs 9–10).
  • Lindsay's explicit sign-off on the KPI/dimension set, the 90-day window (KPIs 9–10), and the time-band boundaries (Dimensions 11–12).

Accepted limitations

  • No automated cross-system join exists yet — Section 6 of the Requirements document is a side-by-side reading framework, not a computed joint report.
  • No true historical status snapshot exists — KPI 5 is a deliberate approximation (not-yet-archived, not confirmed-active), which includes anyone sitting Inactive the whole time.
  • Any reactivation or reinstatement that occurred before this BRD's data extraction began cannot be recovered retroactively.

15 · Success criteria

  • Lindsay can answer her original Retention and Attrition questions precisely, using named measures with stated formulas, rather than an ambiguous single number.
  • A side-by-side reading of volunteer capacity against animal-side demand is possible without a manual re-derivation each time (Requirements document, Section 6).
  • Every measure is reproducible from its documented formula against the same input data (demonstrated against Appendix A).
  • Known limitations (no history, no confirmed join, contested animal-side denominator) are visible in the governing documents, not hidden from the reader.
  • A volunteer-side historical trend exists, once Better Impact's historical depth is confirmed, enabling a benchmark discussion grounded in real pattern rather than a guess.
