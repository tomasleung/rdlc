01 · Executive summary

ShelterBuddy's Source and Status dropdown lists (58 source types, 120 statuses) have grown for 20 years with no governing structure. A stakeholder-led review (Michelle & Cynthia, September 2026) has proposed consolidating 87 "active" statuses down to roughly 6 — built from manual team judgment, not from the system's own 20-year usage history.

This artifact proposes a diagnostic capability, not a consolidation plan: an evidence pack showing which Source types and Status values have actually been used, what real operational paths they follow, and where the current taxonomy is structurally incomplete. Two concrete findings already surfaced during intake: the proposed 6-bucket consolidation covers Centre Operation only — Outgoing (roughly a third of the full status taxonomy) and a distinct Vet/Medical path aren't represented at all — and at least three statuses (Deceased, Returned Home, Privately Rehomed) are inconsistently flagged against the system's own IsOutgoing logic, a setup gap independent of any consolidation decision.

This supports one decision: how ShelterBuddy's status/substatus taxonomy should be consolidated and formalized as an Operational Group SOP. It does not propose or finalize that taxonomy itself — that stays a management judgment, grounded in real operational need. Intended delivery is a static HTML analysis pack, reviewed once with the decision owner(s), not a recurring monitored report.

Interpretation boundary
This is diagnostic evidence, not a KPI or Signal artifact. It carries no ongoing threshold, no Red/Amber/Green rating, and does not itself define the target Operational Group structure.

02 · Business problem

Current condition. Source and Status are flat ShelterBuddy dropdown lists with no grouping or category field on either table. Any status can be selected after any other, with nothing in the system documenting which transitions are operationally valid.

Information gap. Michelle & Cynthia's consolidation proposal was built from team review of the current list, not from what the data shows actually happens. It cannot show whether a status marked for deletion is genuinely unused, or whether a kept status actually represents an under-served operational need (e.g. Vet/Medical, which has no status of its own and is scattered as substatus entries under Hold and Available for Adoption).

People/process affected. Centre operations staff who select statuses daily, the SB Data Owner who must approve any taxonomy change, and every downstream RDLC artifact (Phase 1 reporting, the Phase 2 Centre & Animal Type Utilization BRD, and the Animal-in-Care data-quality work) that depends on Status History to determine in-care vs. outgoing and close care episodes.

Consequence. A consolidation decided without this evidence risks deleting a status the data shows is load-bearing, merging two statuses that represent operationally distinct paths, or leaving the same ungoverned-growth conditions in place for the next 20 years. The IsOutgoing flag gaps found during intake (§12) are a concrete example of a defect that would persist either way unless surfaced here.

Why existing work is insufficient. The existing Intake Source Types and Care Pathways v0.1 draft already does this analysis at the Source level; nothing equivalent exists for Status, and no document currently compares Michelle & Cynthia's proposal against either.

04 · Current state

Existing process. Status/source taxonomy changes are currently proposed through manual stakeholder review (Michelle & Cynthia's September 2026 document), organized by department consultation (Behaviour & Welfare, APS, Wildlife, Home Beyond Ours, Foster Team, Vet Services), with no systematic check against historical usage.

Existing trusted definitions. The Phase 1 and Phase 2 (Centre & Animal Type Utilization) BRDs both describe Status History conceptually — "determines In Care versus Outgoing and closes care episodes" — but neither documents the granular status list, its groupings, or its valid transitions.

Current sources. source.csv and status.csv (ShelterBuddy admin exports, reference/dimension lists only); the draft Intake Source Types and Care Pathways v0.1 document (Source-level empirical analysis, already in progress); the Care Timeline Data Quality Report v0.3 (a related but differently-scoped Status History diagnostic, built for care-episode normality rather than operational grouping).

Known limitations. No event-level Status History extract (Animal ID + Status + timestamp) has been assembled yet for this specific purpose — see §9 and §12. The "87 active statuses" figure in the prior review does not reconcile to any single flag in status.csv (67 rows have IsInCare=True, not 87); the source of that count is unconfirmed.

Manual effort and fragmentation. Status grouping currently exists only as a one-off document produced through meetings, not as a governed, re-checkable artifact.

Must remain authoritative. Phase 1's delivered foster reconciliation figures and the Phase 2 CATU BRD's measure definitions are not in scope for revision here; this artifact must not propose a change to how Status History is used to determine In Care vs. Outgoing in either.

05 · Desired capability

Not "reduce the number of statuses." The target capability is to show management, from 20 years of actual ShelterBuddy history, which Source types and Status values are genuinely used and which aren't, what the real operational paths look like today, and where the current taxonomy is structurally incomplete — so that any consolidation decision is checked against real usage, and the resulting SOP defines complete operational paths (Intake → Centre Operation → Outgoing, with named tracks within Centre Operation), not just a shorter list of atomic values.

  • Source-type usage and care-pathway classification, reusing and extending the existing draft work.
  • Status-value usage frequency across the full 120-row list, not just the subset Michelle & Cynthia's document covered.
  • An explicit 3-stage structural map (Intake / Centre Operation / Outgoing), grounded in the system's own IsIncoming / IsInCare / IsOutgoing flags.
  • A side-by-side comparison of the proposed 6-bucket consolidation against that full structural map, naming the gaps explicitly (Outgoing, Vet/Medical).
  • Disclosure of setup/flag inconsistencies found in the source data itself, independent of the consolidation question.

07 · Analytical approach

  • Reporting grain: Source type and Status value (reference-list grain); Animal-level care pathway matching where reused from the existing pathway document.
  • Population: all incoming intakes, January 2006 to latest available extract (497,158 per the existing pathway document; to be refreshed against the latest extract date before publication).
  • Entity/event identifiers: Animal ID, Source Type Id, Status Id.
  • Time zone: Vancouver local, consistent with prior BRD precedent (Phase 1, Phase 2 CATU).
  • Effective-dated treatment: status changes are effective-dated events; a transition is derived by ordering an animal's status records chronologically — same effective-dating discipline already established for Care Episode logic.
  • Distinct-count keys / additive behaviour: not applicable — this artifact does not define a governed rate or ratio measure (see §8).
  • Dimensional attribution: Source Type → Care Pathway group (6 proposed, from the existing draft); Status → Stage (Intake / Centre Operation / Outgoing) + Operational Group (pending management definition, out of scope for this artifact to assign).
  • Current/open record treatment: an animal with no closing or outgoing status yet is treated as currently in care, consistent with the Rule 4 logic already established in the Animal-in-Care data-quality work — not redefined here.
  • Historical restatement: not applicable — one-time diagnostic, not a recurring governed dataset.
Worked example
Deceased (status 110), Returned Home (93), and Privately Rehomed – New Owner Unknown (125) are all semantically terminal states — an animal reaching any of them has left care — yet none is flagged IsOutgoing=True, unlike comparable statuses such as Euthanized or Unassisted Death, which are. An animal landing on one of these three statuses today would not close its care episode the way a comparable status does.

10 · Data-design requirements

  • Required output grain: one row per status-change event, ordered per animal, once the Status History extract is available.
  • Effective start/end semantics: reuse the Care Episode effective-dating convention already established in the Phase 1/Phase 2 BRDs — not redefined here.
  • Event precedence / tie-breaking: same-day multiple status changes — ordering rule not yet defined. Open
  • Source identifiers retained for audit: Animal ID, Status Id, source record identifier (when available).
  • Change detection: a transition is any pair of consecutive status records for the same animal where Status Id differs.
  • Current-row handling: the most recent status before the extract date represents current state.
  • Unknown/conflict handling: reuse the Rule 1–4 pattern already established in the Animal-in-Care data-quality work (no-status, backdated-status) rather than re-deriving new rules.
  • Lineage/reconciliation: not applicable — this is a one-time diagnostic, not a governed recurring dataset.

13 · Report and consumption requirements

  • Required views: Source usage summary; Status usage summary (full 120); 3-stage/path coverage comparison; setup-inconsistency findings.
  • Filters/comparisons: by care-pathway group; by stage (Intake/Centre Operation/Outgoing).
  • Narrative rules: descriptive only — no causal, performance, or capacity language, consistent with this project's standing interpretation-boundary convention.
  • Zero/unknown presentation: sources or statuses with zero historical usage are named explicitly, not silently omitted.
  • Delivery format: static HTML analysis pack, matching this project's established mock/report pattern.
  • Publication frequency: one-time; republished only if explicitly re-requested, not on a refresh schedule.
  • Accessibility: contrast, alt text, and no color-only signals, per this project's established accessibility standard.

14 · Data quality and validation

  • Validity check: cross-check source.csv/status.csv row counts and flag values against SB's live admin configuration before publishing final figures.
  • Source-to-output reconciliation: Source and Status usage totals must reconcile to the existing Intake Source Types and Care Pathways document's published totals (497,158 intakes) wherever the two overlap.
  • Worked-example validation: the three IsOutgoing flag-inconsistency findings (§7 worked example) serve as the validation case for the flag-consistency audit.
  • Exception handling: any Source or Status with zero historical usage is flagged explicitly, never silently dropped from the published list.
  • Publication blocking criterion: final consolidated recommendations are not published until the Status History transition data is available, or its absence is explicitly accepted by the decision owner as a carried-forward limitation.

15 · Success criteria

  • Management can see, for every Source type and Status value, its actual historical usage and path context.
  • The gap between the proposed 6-bucket consolidation and a complete 3-stage operational path is visible and explicit, not implied.
  • All identified setup/flag inconsistencies (minimum: the 3 IsOutgoing gaps) are raised to the SB Data Owner.
  • Management has the evidence needed to define the Operational Group taxonomy themselves — this artifact has not pre-decided it for them.
Interpretation boundary
Success is measured by whether this evidence changes or confirms management's decision-making — not by any ongoing number, since this artifact establishes no Signal or threshold (§6/Q6 of the intake session, carried forward here).

  • Governance approval: the decision owner (Pending) confirms this diagnostic's findings are accurate and sufficient to proceed to a taxonomy-definition session.
  • Definition approval: the SB Data Owner confirms source.csv/status.csv reflect the current live configuration.

D. Glossary

  • Stage — one of Intake, Centre Operation, or Outgoing; derived from IsIncoming/IsInCare/IsOutgoing.
  • Operational Group — a department-level grouping within Centre Operation (e.g. Behaviour, Vet/Medical, APS/Legal, Foster) — not yet formally defined; management's decision.
  • Path — an ordered sequence of statuses an animal actually moves through, as opposed to an isolated atomic status value.
