Why we offer two services

Not all departments have the same needs, skills, or desired level of Data Team involvement. Some departments want to build and maintain their own analytics solutions. Other departments need the Data Team to build and operate trusted enterprise data products that can be shared across the organization. To support both scenarios, the Data Team offers two service models.

Service 1 — Self-Service Data Platform

Purpose

Provide a secure and governed Microsoft Fabric environment that enables departments to independently build, operate, and maintain their own analytics solutions.

Responsibilities

IT / Data Team — provides and manages the platform

Platform Provisioning

  • Microsoft Fabric capacity
  • Shared or dedicated capacity assignment
  • Workspace creation
  • Workspace lifecycle management

Security & Access

  • Entra security groups
  • Workspace role assignment
  • Access approval process
  • Periodic access reviews

Governance & Compliance

  • Sensitive data classification requirements
  • Security requirements
  • Compliance requirements
  • Platform usage policies

Operations

  • Capacity monitoring
  • Cost monitoring and reporting
  • Capacity alerts
  • Platform health monitoring
  • Microsoft Fabric platform support

Business — owns everything inside the workspace

  • Data ingestion
  • Pipelines
  • Notebooks
  • Lakehouses
  • Warehouses
  • Data models
  • Semantic models
  • Business rules
  • Data quality
  • Reports and dashboards
  • Testing and validation
  • Production support
  • Solution maintenance

Outcome

A governed Microsoft Fabric environment that enables departments to independently build and operate analytics solutions.

What IT does not provide

Under the Self-Service Data Platform model, the Data Team does not:

  • Build pipelines
  • Build data models
  • Design solution architecture
  • Build semantic models
  • Build dashboards or reports
  • Manage data quality
  • Develop business logic
  • Support department-owned analytics solutions

Why Service 1 exists

Benefits

For Business

  • Fast onboarding
  • Maximum flexibility
  • Full ownership
  • No dependency on Data Team delivery schedules

For IT

  • Low operational effort
  • Scalable model
  • Supports multiple departments
  • Clear ownership boundaries

Challenges

For Business

  • Requires technical resources
  • Requires Fabric expertise
  • Responsible for solution support
  • Responsible for data quality

For IT

  • Limited visibility into solutions
  • Potential duplication across departments
  • Less standardization across the organization

Service 2 — Managed Data Product (RDLC)

Purpose

Design, build, and maintain trusted enterprise data products that provide reusable, governed, and organization-wide data assets for Business Intelligence, analytics, and decision making.

Responsibilities

IT / Data Team delivers

  • Source system integration
  • Data ingestion pipelines
  • Data quality controls
  • Identity matching
  • Business rules
  • Enterprise data modeling
  • Curated Gold datasets
  • Certified semantic models
  • Governance and security controls
  • Monitoring and operational support
  • Reusable enterprise data assets

Business provides

  • Business ownership
  • Subject matter expertise
  • Business definitions
  • Validation and testing
  • Prioritization of requirements
  • Data product stewardship
  • Report development and consumption

Outcome

A trusted, governed, reusable enterprise data product that can be consumed by multiple teams across BC SPCA.

Alignment with the RDLC framework

Managed Data Products are intended to support BC SPCA's analytical maturity model:

LayerQuestion
Layer 1What happened?
Layer 2Did something unusual happen?
Layer 3What is related?
Layer 4What is driving it?
Layer 5How much should we trust it?
Layer 6What should we focus on?
Layer 7What should we do?

The Data Team's primary focus is delivering the foundation required for Layers 1 through 5. Layers 6 and 7 are typically developed collaboratively with business stakeholders.

  • Raiser's Edge
  • Identity matching
  • Supporter timeline
  • Engagement history

Foster Analytics Data Product

Benefits

For the Business

  • Trusted data
  • Consistent definitions
  • Reduced technical complexity
  • Faster access to insights
  • Reusable data assets

For IT

  • Centralized governance
  • Standardized architecture
  • Reusable components
  • Better security and compliance

For BC SPCA

  • Single source of truth
  • Consistent KPIs
  • Reduced duplication
  • Cross-department analytics
  • Higher data quality

Challenges

For the Business

  • Longer delivery timelines
  • Prioritization required
  • Greater dependence on Data Team capacity

For IT

  • Higher development effort
  • Ongoing operational responsibility
  • Requires strong governance and ownership models

Recommended positioning

The Data Team's preferred strategic investment is the Managed Data Product model. While the Self-Service Data Platform enables departmental innovation, Managed Data Products create trusted, reusable, organization-wide data assets that maximize the value of BC SPCA's data and align with the RDLC framework.
