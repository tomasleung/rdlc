Business Problem Statement
Current Problem
The Data Team becomes a bottleneck because:
Every new data source requires Data Team involvement.
Departments wait in a delivery queue.
Small requests compete with enterprise initiatives.
Business users cannot easily experiment with new data sources.
The Data Team spends time on low-value integration work instead of enterprise data products.
Business Objective
Enable departments to independently discover, ingest, transform, and analyze departmental data while operating within a governed Microsoft Fabric environment.
ROI Drivers
1. Faster Time to Insight
Current State
Department requests Data Team support.
Solution enters backlog.
Development waits for prioritization.
Future State
Department receives a Fabric workspace.
Bronze data is available.
Department builds Silver and Gold layers independently.
Expected Benefit
Reduce analytics delivery time from months to weeks.
Faster experimentation and learning.
2. Reduce Data Team Bottlenecks
Instead of building:
Mailchimp
SurveyMonkey
Volunteer systems
One-off departmental datasets
The Data Team focuses on:
Fabric governance
Shared platforms
Enterprise data products
Certified datasets
This is directly aligned with the service model's separation of responsibilities.
3. Increase Data Adoption
Many departments never start because the effort is too high.
With self-service:
Teams can access data sooner.
More users become data consumers.
More business-led innovation occurs.
4. Lower Cost of Analytics Delivery
Rather than creating custom solutions for every department:
One platform → Many departments
The same:
Fabric capacity
Governance framework
Security model
Workspace process
supports multiple use cases.
5. Create a Pipeline for Future Data Products
This is the most important strategic ROI.
Many self-service initiatives will eventually reveal enterprise needs.
Example:
Phase 1
Marketing wants Mailchimp data.
Data Team lands Mailchimp into Bronze.

Multiple teams begin consuming the data.
At this stage it becomes a candidate for a Managed Data Product.
Self-service becomes the incubation layer for future enterprise data products.

Asana Epic Description
Epic: Self-Service Data Platform Foundation
Problem: Departments depend on the Data Team to onboard and analyze every new data source, creating delivery bottlenecks and slowing business innovation.
Objective: Provide a governed Microsoft Fabric platform where the Data Team manages provisioning, governance, security, and Bronze ingestion patterns while departments independently develop Silver, Gold, semantic models, and reporting.
Business Outcome: Faster access to data, reduced delivery bottlenecks, improved scalability, and a pathway for identifying future enterprise data products.
For BC SPCA specifically, a strong example is Mailchimp: the Data Team owns the API onboarding and Bronze ingestion, while Marketing owns the business transformations in Silver and downstream analytics. If Mailchimp later becomes integrated with Raiser's Edge and consumed across multiple departments, it graduates from Self-Service into a Managed Data Product.
