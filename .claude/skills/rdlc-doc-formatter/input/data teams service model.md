
BC SPCA Data Team Service Model
Why We Offer Two Services
Not all departments have the same needs, skills, or desired level of Data Team involvement.
Some departments want to build and maintain their own analytics solutions.
Other departments need the Data Team to build and operate trusted enterprise data products that can be shared across the organization.
To support both scenarios, the Data Team offers two service models.
Service 1: Self-Service Data Platform
Purpose
Provide a governed Microsoft Fabric environment that enables departments to independently build and maintain their own analytics solutions while operating within BC SPCA security, governance, and compliance standards.
IT / Data Team Responsibilities
The Data Team provides and governs the platform:
Microsoft Fabric capacity management
Workspace provisioning
Security and access management
Entra security group configuration
Governance and compliance standards
Sensitive data classification and controls
Platform monitoring and operational support
Capacity usage and cost monitoring
Platform standards and best practices
Business Responsibilities
The business team owns the analytics solution:
Data ingestion and integration
Data pipelines and transformations
Data modeling
Business rules and calculations
Reports and dashboards
Solution testing and validation
Ongoing maintenance and enhancement
Business support and training
Outcome
A governed Microsoft Fabric environment that enables departments to independently build, operate, and evolve their own analytics solutions.
What IT Does Not Provide
Under the Self-Service Data Platform model, the Data Team does not:
Build pipelines on behalf of departments
Design department-specific data models
Develop dashboards or reports
Maintain department-owned analytics solutions
Perform ongoing data quality management for department-owned datasets
Act as business analysts or report developers
Benefits
For the Business
Greater flexibility
Faster experimentation
Full ownership of the solution
Reduced dependency on the Data Team
For IT
Lower ongoing support effort
Scalable operating model
Enables innovation across departments
Challenges
For the Business

Requires dedicated resources
Higher ownership burden
Risk of inconsistent data definitions
For IT

Possible duplication of effort across departments
Harder to standardize metrics and KPIs
Service 2: Managed Data Product (RDLC)
Purpose
Design, build, and maintain trusted enterprise data products that provide reusable, governed, and organization-wide data assets for Business Intelligence, analytics, and decision making.
IT / Data Team Responsibilities
The Data Team delivers:
Source system integration
Data ingestion pipelines
Data quality controls
Identity matching
Business rules
Enterprise data modeling
Curated Gold datasets
Certified semantic models
Governance and security controls
Monitoring and operational support
Reusable enterprise data assets
Business Responsibilities
The business team provides:
Business ownership
Subject matter expertise
Business definitions
Validation and testing
Prioritization of requirements
Data product stewardship
Report development and consumption
Outcome
A trusted, governed, reusable enterprise data product that can be consumed by multiple teams across BC SPCA.
Alignment with the RDLC Framework
Managed Data Products are intended to support BC SPCA's analytical maturity model:
Layer	QuestionLayer 1	What happened?
Layer 2	Did something unusual happen?
Layer 3	What is related?
Layer 4	What is driving it?
Layer 5	How much should we trust it?
Layer 6	What should we focus on?
Layer 7	What should we do?
The Data Team's primary focus is delivering the foundation required for Layers 1 through 5. Layers 6 and 7 are typically developed collaboratively with business stakeholders.
