01 · Primary business questions

Restated verbatim from BRD §06 — not reinvented.

  1. How many animals are in care by month, Centre, Region, and Animal Type?
  2. How many of those animals experience qualifying foster care?
  3. What share of Animals in Care experience foster care?
  4. Which centres and regions use foster most?
  5. Which animal-type families use foster most?
  6. Where are changes occurring over time?
  7. How often do animals relate to multiple centres in one month?
  8. What data-quality gaps affect interpretation?

02 · Analytical approach

Each Animal-in-Care-month is evaluated once per Centre × Animal Type Family group: did this animal experience a qualifying foster stay (Phase 1's unmodified foster-definition logic) overlapping at least one day of the month? Organization-wide totals are computed independently from dimensional subtotals, never by summing them, because an animal can validly belong to more than one Centre or Animal Type group within the same month (BRD §7.1).

03 · Data used

Reporting Month, Animal ID, Care Episode ID, Effective Centre, Effective Region, Source Animal Type ID/Label, Normalized Animal Type Family, Is Animal in Care, Is Fostered Animal, Foster Days, Unmapped Centre Flag, Unknown Animal Type Flag, Data Conflict Flag — all per BRD §07.1/§09, none added.

04 · Drivers

Only three — the BRD's confirmed in-scope dimensions. Not Species-level, Age Group, or Municipality: all three are explicitly out of scope or deferred per BRD §11 and this project's documented gaps.

Driver 1 — Time

Story: Where are changes occurring over time? (BQ6)

Views: monthly trend from Jan 2006, year-over-year percentage-point change, current-quarter vs. year-to-date.

YoY comparison · Must

Driver 2 — Centre & Region

Story: Which centres/regions use foster most, and is that difference real or just a different animal-type mix? (BQ1, BQ4, BQ7, BQ8)

Views: Centre/Region volume & utilization ranking, Centre × Type mix-adjustment, multiple-centre attribution, Unmapped Centre data-quality view.

Top-N / Pareto · Must Standardization · Prefer

Driver 3 — Animal Type Family

Story: Which families rely most on foster care, and does that explain apparent Centre differences? (BQ5)

Views: Type volume & utilization, Centre × Type intersection.

Kitagawa decomposition · Must

05 · Open question — flagged, not decided here

BRD §06 has no business question resembling Phase 1's "is there seasonality" (Phase 1 Q3). Per non-negotiable rule 8 — never silently copy a prior report's content forward — a seasonal-utilization view (CATU-SEASON-01) has not been added by default. This needs a decision from the Foster Manager / Animal Flow Manager: was seasonality intentionally dropped from Phase 2's scope, or is it a BRD oversight that should be added back?
