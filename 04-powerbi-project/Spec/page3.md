## Executive Insight

```text
Foster participation follows a predictable seasonal pattern across all historical reporting years.

Activity increases through spring, reaches its highest levels between May and August, and remains elevated through early fall before moderating during winter months.

The consistency of this seasonal cycle provides opportunities for proactive foster recruitment, volunteer engagement, and operational capacity planning before peak demand begins.

Historical benchmarking indicates that June is the highest-demand month, while February is typically the lowest-demand month.
```

✗ Do not repeat Page 1 KPIs

## Key Business Message

```text
The Foster Program exhibits a stable and predictable seasonal cycle.

Operational planning should focus on preparing foster capacity ahead of the May-August peak activity period rather than reacting to demand after it occurs.
```
