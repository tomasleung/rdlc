Executive Briefing

Foster Program Executive Assessment

The Foster Program experienced significant expansion between 2006 and 2010 before transitioning into a mature and stable operating model. Annual foster participation increased from 5,223 animals in 2006 to a peak of 15,763 in 2010. Since then, annual activity has generally remained within the 13,000–15,000 range, indicating a predictable and sustainable level of demand. Historical analysis confirms recurring seasonal peaks between May and August, while recent performance remains aligned with expected operating levels. Based on year-to-date activity through August 2026, the program is projected to support approximately 12,800 fostered animals by year-end.

Definitions, Methodology & Trust Center

Methodology, Benchmarks & Forecast Validation

Transparent definitions and operational guidelines for program owners, directors, and analysts.

1. Primary Reporting Metric: Foster Animal Count

Metric

Foster Animal Count

Count of distinct animals that were in foster at any point during the reporting period (month or year). An animal placed in foster more than once during the same reporting period is counted once.

Reporting Logic

Distinct Animal, Per Period

Tracked by unique animal identifier per month or calendar year touched.

Purpose

Measures Program Reach

Represents unique animals receiving foster support and is used for growth analysis, seasonality benchmarking, forecasting, and executive reporting.

2. Benchmark Methodology (How Benchmarks Are Calculated)

Monthly benchmarks are calculated using the average foster participation from the five most recent complete reporting years (2021–2025). This represents expected activity levels during the current mature operating period.

Example: August Benchmark Calculation

2021: 9,285 | 2022: 9,473 | 2023: 9,636 | 2024: 9,417 | 2025: 9,362

5-Year August Average = 9,435

Performance Status Rules

🟢 On Target Within ±10% of benchmark
🟡 Monitor Between 10% and 15% variance
🔴 Action Required Greater than 15% variance

Historical analysis shows that approximately 88.5% of annual foster participation has typically occurred by the end of August. This completion ratio pattern is used to estimate full-year participation for 2026.

Why You Can Trust This Dashboard

The dashboard uses Foster Animal Count as its primary reporting metric because it represents the number of unique animals receiving foster care. Monthly benchmarks are based on recent historical participation patterns (2021–2025), while year-end projections use Historical Ratio Forecasting based on historical August completion rates.

Historical validation produced an average forecast error of approximately ±0.9%, demonstrating that the forecasting methodology performs well against completed reporting years. Together, these methods provide a consistent, transparent, and evidence-based foundation for monitoring performance and planning future foster program operations.
