# Q2 Visualization Specification

## Title

Q2. Is There Seasonality?

## Executive Answer

Yes. Peak foster activity consistently occurs between May and August.

## Purpose

Provide visual evidence that:

1. Foster activity follows a predictable seasonal pattern.
2. Activity rises through spring.
3. Peak activity occurs between May and August.
4. Activity moderates during fall and winter.
5. Seasonality can be used for operational planning and foster recruitment.

---

## Recommended Visual Type

Area Chart

### Style

- Smooth seasonal curve
- Orange seasonal theme
- Light orange area fill under line
- Highlight peak season window (May-August)
- Minimal data labels
- Focus on storytelling, not every data point

## Executive Insight Box

```text
Foster participation follows a predictable seasonal pattern.
```

---

## Visual Design Rules

✓ Use area chart

✓ Use orange seasonal theme

✓ Highlight May-August peak season

✓ Highlight June (Peak Month)

✓ Highlight February (Lowest Month)

✓ Display Peak Month, Lowest Month, and Seasonal Range metrics

✓ Include executive insight box

✓ Prioritize storytelling over technical detail

✗ Do not show all monthly data labels

✗ Do not clutter chart with benchmark calculations

✗ Do not repeat KPI values from Page 1

✗ Do not display individual year data
