## Executive Conclusion

Replace bullet list with a highlighted conclusion box.

### Executive Conclusion Text

```text
The Foster Program has transitioned from a period of rapid growth into a mature and stable operating model.

Most program growth occurred between 2006 and 2010. Since then, annual foster participation has remained relatively stable, while foster activity continues to follow a predictable seasonal pattern with peak participation occurring between May and August.

Historical Ratio Forecasting projects approximately 12,800 fostered animals for 2026, which is nearly identical to 2025 actual performance (12,778). Historical forecast validation produced an average error of approximately ±0.9%, providing confidence that the Foster Program will continue to operate within its established mature operating range.
```
