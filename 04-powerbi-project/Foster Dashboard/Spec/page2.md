## Section 4

Executive Insight

```text
The Foster Program underwent rapid expansion between
2006 and 2010, leading to a peak activity period
between 2010 and 2014.

Although annual participation has moderated slightly
from its historical peak, program activity has
remained remarkably stable over the last decade.

The current Mature Program Phase (2020–2025)
supported 36,146 foster animals, which is only
6.9% below the historical peak era.

These results indicate that the Foster Program
has successfully transitioned from a growth phase
into a mature and sustainable operating model.
```
