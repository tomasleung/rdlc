1. Primary Reporting Metric: Foster Animal Count

Core Metric

Business Definition

Count of distinct animals that were actively in foster care at any point during the selected reporting period (month or year).

Why This Metric Was Chosen

Historically, reporting focused heavily on transactional intakes rather than active care utilization. Foster Animal Count was chosen as the primary organizational metric because it directly measures program reach—revealing exactly how many unique animals received foster support.

Important Interpretation Notes

  • Distinct Counting: An animal is counted once per reporting period. If active across multiple months, it appears in each month's count.
  • No Simple Summation: Monthly values must not be summed for annual totals to avoid double-counting.
  • Not Intakes: Measures active program demand and reach, not raw intake events or placement episodes.

2. Operational Benchmarks & Forecasting Logic

Seasonal Benchmark Methodology

Monthly benchmarks represent expected activity levels during our current mature operating period. They are calculated using the average foster participation from the five most recent completed reporting years (2021–2025). Program owners use these baselines to monitor monthly variances and guide seasonal capacity planning.

Forecast Methodology (Ratio Forecasting)

To project year-end performance without waiting for complete annual data, we apply historical completion ratios. On average, approximately 88.5% of annual foster participation occurs by the end of August. Dividing August year-to-date actuals by this completion rate gives our year-end projection.

Why You Can Trust This Dashboard

This dashboard establishes a single source of truth for foster operations by pairing a rigorous distinct-count metric with historical seasonal baselines. To ensure complete reliability, our forecasting methodology is continuously tested against completed historical years (2021–2025), consistently producing a minor average forecast error of ±0.9%. Program leaders can make resource, volunteer, and capacity decisions with full confidence in these numbers.
