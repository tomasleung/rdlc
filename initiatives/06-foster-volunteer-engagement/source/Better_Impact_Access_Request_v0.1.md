# Better Impact Access Request — Starting Point

**Author:** Tomas Leung. **Collaborators named:** Maggie Stewart (Animal Flow team) — the same Maggie Stewart named as Product Owner on `BRD_Foster_Analysis_Phase1_Counters_v1_1.docx` (initiative 01). **Saved to repo:** 2026-11-18, as the objective/starting-point document for this initiative, per Tomas's explicit instruction.

---

I'm working with @Maggie Stewart and the Animal Flow team on improving our animal pathway and foster-care reporting. To support this work, I'd like to request read-only access to Better Impact so I can analyze foster volunteer data alongside our ShelterBuddy animal-care data.

Today we can see the animal-care side through ShelterBuddy, including animals in care and pathway activity. Access to Better Impact would provide visibility into foster volunteer participation and capacity.

This will help us better understand:
- The relationship between animals in care and available foster volunteers.
- Opportunities to increase the percentage of animals placed in foster care.
- Foster volunteer retention and engagement trends.
- Foster capacity planning and future needs.

We're also interested in developing foster-retention KPIs and understanding the balance between:
- Need: animals in our care that may require foster placement.
- Capacity: available and active foster volunteers.

Having both ShelterBuddy and Better Impact data will help us better understand whether we have enough foster capacity to support the animals entering care, where gaps may exist, and how volunteer retention impacts our ability to place animals in foster homes.

Read-only access will be sufficient for my needs.

Please let me know if there is an approval process 😉

---

## Why this matters beyond the access request itself

This email uses **"animals in care"** and **"percentage of animals placed in foster care"** as its own framing — language that directly overlaps with the already-contested Phase 1 measure and CATU's `% Animals in Care Fostered` (initiatives 01 and 02). This initiative is not actually independent of those two: it explicitly wants to relate foster-volunteer capacity to the same "Animals in Care" population CATU already defines. Before any KPI gets confirmed here, check it against the measures already defined in initiatives 01/02 — this is exactly the kind of cross-initiative naming collision this project has already hit twice (see `PROJECT_STATUS.md`'s naming note).
